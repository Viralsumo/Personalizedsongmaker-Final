1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

  • Personal Data: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. This includes the details you provide in the song creation form (e.g., name, occasion, details) and your email address if you choose to receive a backup.

We use the collected data for various purposes:

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with third-party service providers to facilitate our Service:

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they use such information.
